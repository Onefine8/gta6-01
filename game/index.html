Port Meridian — drive it, steal it, own it.

Rook is a getaway driver with no crew and one fixer: Mara Voss. Dante Kroll owns the harbor — and he wants Rook gone. Eight jobs stand between Rook and the top of Port Meridian.

How to play

  • WASD — walk / drive   Shift — run   Space — jump
  • E — steal / enter a car   Mouse — aim & shoot (click to fire)
  • R — radio   H — horn   C — camera   Esc — pause
  • Follow the waypoint. Lose the cops. Finish all 8 missions.
